# Zonal FR Review — source-driven period

Reference: PFA FR AUG 2026- with charts consolidated.pptx (23 slides). The August figures supply the reference structure; current figures come from the local source workbooks.

OWE views follow slides 2, 5–12: Zone performance, Demand-wise OWE, six Divisions (DLI 0303, FZR 0304, JAT 0305, LKO 0306, MB 0307, UMB 0325), and controllable PUs 10/11/12/15/16/26/28/32. Data is taken from NR_ZONE_DATA using completed fiscal months through the month before the running month. Values convert Rs thousands to crore by dividing by 10000. Operational OWE includes Demands 03–13; Suspense 12N/10N is separate. The completed-months / 12 annual-budget comparison is an even-phasing benchmark, not official source BP.

The controllable watch list selects the two highest-utilisation Divisions for each listed PU and shows all six Division actuals separately. Zero-budget spend ranks as needing review; utilisation is unavailable for nonpositive budgets.

The six-Division summary adds Other AUs = Zone less six Divisions, then shows the Zone total without adding that total again.

Revenue, operating ratio and CAPEX remain pending until matching data is supplied. Previous-year OWE comparisons use the supplied scope-matched data. The main scope dropdown is kept consistent with the selected FR subview. FR Review, Expenditure Monitoring and AI Insight advance with the shared source reporting period. Exports capture the current subview and its period.
Monitoring Expenditure compares PU, Department, Demand and Month for Zone plus six Divisions, all AUs, or two chosen scopes. Head filters apply to the comparison. Annual utilisation colours are green at or below the completed-month benchmark, yellow above that benchmark through 100%, red above 100%; monthly benchmark colours are green <=100%, yellow >100–120%, red >120%. Missing and nonpositive budgets remain unavailable for utilisation. Zone/AU amounts are never added together.
Expenditure Monitoring now defaults to Zone only and one head. All AUs excludes the zonal total. Selected AUs and Zone + selected AUs support multiple AU choices. Multiple PU/Department/Demand/Month choices are retained in report metadata. Graphs offers Bar, Line and Heat map presentation styles for the existing selected analysis; future unreported months remain unavailable.

FR Review: Every graph offers Bar chart, Line chart and Heat map. AU and head selections use searchable checkbox dropdowns. Comparisons use numeric head/AU order and fiscal month order. AI Insight offers Alerts, Look out, Monitoring and AI comments/insights using the independent source-period selection and explicit rules. Missing data is marked unavailable. No external AI service is used.

FR pages are separated into Review, Expenditure Monitoring and AI Insight. Only the active page controls/content are shown. The shared main report filter bar is hidden on Zonal FR Review. Controls use compact spacing.

AI Insight has independent filters: default Zone only, Alerts and All dimensions; choose PU, Department, Demand or Month, All AUs or selected AUs, and checkbox heads/months. Monitoring selections do not feed insight records. Hidden FR page tables/charts are excluded from exports. Inactive portal panels are explicitly hidden.

FY 2025-26 scope-matched data is loaded for comparisons. Each financial review includes matching completed-month PY/CY actuals, movement and YoY. AI comments include matching PY amounts and changes. Export comparison produces a compact native-table PPT without including other page tables. The previous-year departmental budget row-limit gap remains unavailable.
